# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Critique code and evidence, not people.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, impersonation, and publication of private
information are not welcome. Never post a real CV, employer application, credential,
or private conversation as a reproduction.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, ask participants to revise it, or restrict
participation. Decisions should be proportionate and explained when that does not
expose private information. For urgent harassment or private-data exposure, use
GitHub's private abuse-reporting controls. Ordinary project concerns may be raised
in a sanitized issue without naming or exposing affected people.
