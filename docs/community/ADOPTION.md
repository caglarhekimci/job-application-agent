## Counting rules

- Count independent people, not runs, devices, downloads, stars, CI jobs, bots, or maintainer tests.
- Record a successful installation separately from a completed workflow.
- Record a blocked installation; do not remove failures to improve the rate.
- A friend or collaborator may be a pilot participant, but describe recruitment accurately and do not present it as broad community adoption.
- Quote or name a person only with explicit publication permission.
- Never request a real CV for public evidence. Pilot users should begin with the synthetic demo and keep any real data in their own private environment.
